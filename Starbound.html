Starbound

The Challenge

The challenge was to design a mobile app experience to support ramp agents in tracking and completing turnaround tasks in real-time. The design needed to accommodate dynamic airfield conditions—outdoor environments, time-critical tasks, and team-based coordination. The core challenge was to streamline complex operational workflows into a simple, accessible interface while ensuring safety, accountability, and clear communication throughout every stage of a shift.

Product: Mobile App

My Role

As the sole designer on this design challenge, I was responsible for the complete end-to-end design process—from research and user persona development to wireframing, prototyping, and high-fidelity UI design in Figma. Every decision was made to balance real-time efficiency, task clarity, and safety considerations, with mobile usability and intuitive design at the forefront.


Tools: Figma & Photoshop
