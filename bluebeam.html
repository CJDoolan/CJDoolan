UX/UI About me

Bluebeam Software

The Challenge

Bluebeam University and the Pre-Portal training platform struggled with inconsistent styling, complex navigation, and low user engagement—especially among users with limited tech experience. The fragmented interface made learning more frustrating than seamless. Our mission was to transform the platform into a visually cohesive, user-friendly experience that aligns with Bluebeam’s branding while making navigation effortless and content more engaging.

Duration: 4 weeks (full time)

My Role

As the Lead Web Developer & Co-Web Designer, I played a key role in revamping the interface, ensuring a sleek, intuitive design that simplifies the user journey. I focused on enhancing accessibility, streamlining navigation, and implementing a design system that brings consistency and clarity to Bluebeam’s educational platforms.

Tools: Figma, Skilljar, Illustrator, CSS, & JQuery
