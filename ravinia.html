The Challenge

The Ravinia College Consultant website was cluttered, hard to navigate, and difficult to use on mobile. Its overwhelming color scheme and disorganized content made finding essential information a frustrating experience. To fix this, we introduced a sleek, responsive design, restructured key sections, and refined the homepage for better readability. Testimonials were categorized, and lengthy text blocks were broken down for a smoother user experience.

Duration: 10 months (part-time)

My Role

As Lead Web Developer, Lead Designer, and Project Manager, I spearheaded the redesign, ensuring a seamless, visually cohesive, and user-friendly platform. From refining the site’s structure to optimizing accessibility, I transformed the website into an intuitive and engaging resource for students and parents.

Tools: Figma, GoDaddy, Illustrator, HTML, CSS, & Javascript
