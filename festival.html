UX/UI About me

LA Food Festival

The Challenge

The LA Food Festival attracts thousands of food lovers, but navigating the event can be overwhelming. Attendees need real-time updates, easy vendor access, and seamless event navigation—all in one place. Our team took on the challenge of designing an intuitive, all-in-one app to enhance the festival experience for both families and food enthusiasts.

Duration: 2 months

My Role

As a Web Developer, Co-App Designer, and Co-Graphic Designer, I helped craft a user-friendly interface, ensuring smooth navigation and visually appealing layouts. From wireframes to final design, I worked on creating an engaging, functional, and cohesive digital experience for festival-goers.

Tools: Figma, Illustrator, React.js, HTML, & CSS
